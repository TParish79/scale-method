The Real Problem

Growth Problems Are
Easy to Misdiagnose

Revenue has stalled. The owner is working constantly. Leads are inconsistent. Customers are not returning. Growth created more complexity instead of more freedom.

Revenue has stalled or gone flat.
You are working constantly and it never lets up.
Leads come in bursts, not on a rhythm.
Customers buy once and do not return.
Growth added complexity, not freedom.
Every important decision still runs through you.

The natural response is to try more marketing, add another offer, hire someone, or work harder. But the visible problem is not always the real constraint.

A business can have a strong strategy and weak execution. It can generate customers but fail to retain them. It can grow revenue while running short of cash. It can appear successful while every important decision still depends on the owner.

Growth does not average your strengths.
The weakest system becomes the ceiling.
The Diagnosis

Find Where
Growth Is Breaking

The SCALE Method examines five connected areas of the business. You will begin with a baseline SCALE Score, work through all five phases in order, and retake the assessment at Day 30 to see where the business moved and what should happen next.

S
Strategy
Clear goals, a defined market, a meaningful competitive position, and an executable roadmap.
C
Customer
Customer experience, retention, trust, referrals, expansion, and customer economics.
A
Action
Repeatable marketing, sales, offers, partnerships, automation, and cash-flow discipline.
L
Leverage
Processes, delegation, team structure, efficiency, and reducing dependence on the owner.
E
Endurance
Authority, feedback, measurement, operating rhythm, and sustained progress.

Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Does my business need to be a certain size?
Yes. The SCALE Method is designed for established businesses generating $500,000–$3 million in annual revenue. The application uses gross revenue from the most recent 12 months to confirm fit.
How much time should I expect?
Plan for approximately 30–45 minutes per day plus one 60-minute session each week. Some implementation actions may require additional business time.
Do I have to attend every session?
Live attendance is strongly recommended. Participants must attend at least four of the five sessions and complete all five capstones to be recorded as program completers.
What if I fall behind?
Resume rather than restarting. Submit the weekly check-in and identify where you became stuck so the most important work can be prioritized.
Are the sessions recorded?
Instructional portions will be recorded for participant replay. Sensitive participant-specific discussions may not be recorded.
Is this individual consulting?
No. The Sprint provides group implementation guidance. It does not include private calls, unlimited messaging, or detailed review of every workbook page.
What is Ask The Book™?
It is the SCALE AI Advisor. Participants can ask questions about the system, request examples for their industry, clarify exercises, and work through implementation decisions between sessions.
Will this increase my revenue in 30 days?
No particular financial result can be guaranteed. The Sprint is designed to help you diagnose constraints, implement business systems, take measurable action, and establish evidence of progress.
What happens to my business information?
Participant information remains confidential. Scores, quotes, and results are not used publicly without explicit permission.
What is the refund policy?
Because the purchase includes digital materials and reserves one of ten live seats, payment becomes nonrefundable after the materials are accessed or 48 hours after purchase, whichever occurs first. If the Sprint is canceled by T.M. Parish, participants receive a full refund.
Can I continue after the Sprint?
Yes. The included Compound Workbook and AI Advisor carry the system through Day 90. Private 60-day implementation support is also available separately for qualified owners who want individualized guidance.
