What you'll have by the end of Week 1

Seven days. Seven things you can point to.

It all rolls up into your Strategy Blueprint — the one page you sign, date, and run the next 30 days against.

  • A specific, measurable 30-day revenue goal. Not a wish. A target.
  • An honest audit of where your operations break down. The constraints capping your growth today.
  • A crystal-clear ideal customer profile. No more "everyone."
  • Your competitive positioning. Why someone chooses you over the alternative.
  • A day-by-day growth plan. So you know exactly what to do next.
  • Scalable processes that can handle growth. Instead of breaking under it.
  • Your team aligned and clear on the plan. Or your own accountability structure, if you're solo.
